C003

Working Memory Capacity

Core B03 · Executive Function MML-1 · Established ⓘ MML-1: Measurement infrastructure exists. Multiple independent validation studies with consistent results.
Version 1.3.2 · Last revised March 2024 · Core layer
Definition — Baddeley & related

The capacity to hold and manipulate information in mind over short periods — the workbench of conscious thought. Underpins reasoning, comprehension, and the integration of incoming information with stored knowledge. Working memory is distinct from short-term memory in that it involves active processing rather than passive storage.

Poles
Low Pole

Difficulty maintaining active representations under normal cognitive load; information fades before it can be used or integrated. Associated with challenges in multi-step reasoning, reading comprehension, and instruction-following.

High Pole

Sustained manipulation of multiple information streams under high load; flexible recombination and integration even when task demands compete. Associated with advantages in fluid reasoning, complex planning, and novel problem-solving.

Mid-reference

Functional working memory under typical everyday demands; occasional lapses under high load or distraction. The statistically typical range for adults in non-clinical populations.

Measure

◈ Capacity (number of items maintainable), accuracy under load, and decay rate under distraction. Standardised tasks include N-back variants, reading span, and operation span procedures.

Trainability
Mid
Can be improved through deliberate practice, though gains are modest and task-specific.
Modifiability
High
Responsive to a range of interventions including environmental change, therapy, and life events.

Notes

Baddeley & Hitch (1974) multi-component model remains the dominant theoretical framework. Central executive, phonological loop, and visuospatial sketchpad are treated as sub-components rather than separate elements in this framework.
