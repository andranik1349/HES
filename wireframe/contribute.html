01 — Translation · HES Lingua

HES Lingua is the translation programme for the Human Elements System. All 343 element names, definitions, and pole descriptions are being translated into multiple languages to extend the framework's global reach and support cross-cultural research.

02 — Academic Collaboration

Researchers interested in conducting validation studies, cross-cultural adaptations, or applied investigations using the HES framework are encouraged to get in touch. Collaboration may involve co-authorship, data sharing agreements, or advisory arrangements depending on the project scope. The author is particularly interested in multi-site and longitudinal study designs.
