03 — MML Framework
Level Label Description
MML-1
Established
Multiple independent validation studies with consistent results. Robust measurement infrastructure exists.
MML-2
Validated
At least one well-designed study with adequate power. Reliable measurement tools exist but cross-study replication is limited.
MML-3
Emerging
Preliminary evidence exists. Construct has been operationalised and initial studies conducted, but evidence base is thin.
MML-4
Theoretical
Construct defined and theoretically grounded; no validation studies completed. Measurement approaches proposed but untested.
MML-5
Speculative
Proposed construct with theoretical basis only. Included to map the landscape; not yet suitable for applied use.
