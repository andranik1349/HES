The Four Layers

Core

Hardware

106 elements

Stable, foundational capacities — attention, memory, executive control. These elements underlie all higher cognition and change slowly across the lifespan.

Explore Core →

Derived

Software

154 elements

Capacities grown from core through experience — cognitive styles, identity, communication, decision-making. Plastic and shaped by environment.

Quantum

Crash States

40 elements

Discontinuous rupture states — freeze, collapse, overwhelm, dissociation. Short-lived but consequential, and often misread as the layers beneath them.

The workbench of conscious affect — holding and manipulating emotional information across time. Underpins meaning-making and felt learning.
