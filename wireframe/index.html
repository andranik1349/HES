What is HES

Everything a human is made of, catalogued.

HES is a structured reference for the elements that compose human behaviour, cognition and physiology — 343 distinct passports across four layers, from the millisecond reflex to the slow weather of mood. A field guide, not a personality test.

About the Project HES Library

What HES is not

HES is not a personality test, not a diagnostic tool, and not a measurement of who you are.

HES defines itself as much by what it excludes as by what it includes. The Stop List contains constructs deliberately kept out; the Parking Lot contains constructs deferred for future consideration.

The Four Layers

Core

Hardware

106 elements

Stable, foundational capacities — attention, memory, executive control. These elements underlie all higher cognition and change slowly across the lifespan.

Explore Core →

Derived

Software

154 elements

Capacities grown from core through experience — cognitive styles, identity, communication, decision-making. Plastic and shaped by environment.

Quantum

Crash States

40 elements

Discontinuous rupture states — freeze, collapse, overwhelm, dissociation. Short-lived but consequential, and often misread as the layers beneath them.

Emotional

Weather

43 elements

The workbench of conscious affect — holding and manipulating emotional information across time. Underpins meaning-making and felt learning.
