About HES — copyable description
The Human Elements System (HES) is a structured reference framework for the constituent elements of human psychology. It catalogues 343 psychological constructs across four layers — Core, Derived, Quantum, and Emotional — each rated on a Measurement Maturity Level scale from MML-1 (established, multiple independent validation studies) to MML-5 (theoretical basis only). HES is not a personality test or diagnostic instrument. It is a versioned reference architecture for researchers, practitioners, and educators who need to work with psychological constructs precisely.
About the Author — copyable bio
Areg Oganesian is an independent researcher and practitioner working at the intersection of psychological measurement, cognitive science, and applied frameworks for human development. He is the creator of the Human Elements System (HES), a structured reference framework cataloguing 343 psychological constructs developed over 19 years of systematic literature review and clinical practice. He lives and works in Yerevan, Armenia.
