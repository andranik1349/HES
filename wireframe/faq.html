FAQ

Common questions about the HES framework.

No. HES is a reference framework for structured psychological constructs — not a test, not a diagnostic instrument, and not a tool for labelling individuals. It maps the building blocks of human psychology, not types or categories.

MML stands for Measurement Maturity Level. It is a 1–5 scale that indicates the evidential weight behind each element's measurement infrastructure, from MML-1 (Established — multiple independent validation studies) to MML-5 (Speculative — theoretical basis only).

Elements were selected through a systematic review of the psychological literature over 19 years. Each element meets a minimum threshold of construct distinctiveness, theoretical coherence, and at least preliminary measurability.

Core elements are stable, biologically grounded capacities that change slowly across the lifespan — attention, memory, sensory processing. Derived elements grow from the core through experience and culture — communication styles, identity constructs, decision-making patterns.

Quantum states are discontinuous rupture events — freeze, collapse, overwhelm, dissociation — that interrupt ordinary function. They are brief but consequential, and are often misread as stable traits rather than states.

No. HES is explicitly not a clinical tool and does not replace professional psychological, psychiatric, or medical assessment. It is a reference framework for research and educational purposes.

Each layer has its own version number (e.g. Core v1.3.2). Individual passports carry a last-revised date. The changelog at changelog tracks all additions, migrations, and corrections. This mirrors the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy model.

Each element lists up to five other elements with the closest theoretical and empirical relationships. These connections are assigned based on shared variance in validation studies and conceptual proximity in the literature.

Translation is underway via the HES Lingua project. Armenian and Russian are currently in progress. See contribute for the current language status and to volunteer as a translator.

Each passport page includes a citation block with APA and BibTeX formats and a one-click copy button. The canonical URL for each passport is permanent and individually indexable.
